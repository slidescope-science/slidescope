# Getting help with SlideScope

- **Email:** support@slidescope.science
- **In the app:** the Support button in the top-right corner sends a message with the
  diagnostics we need, and you see our replies inside the app.
- **Documentation:** https://slidescope.science/, including the
  [supported formats](https://slidescope.science/en/supported-file-formats/) and the
  [release notes](https://slidescope.science/en/release-notes/).

This repository does not take issues, because a public tracker invites people to post slide
images, and slides can carry patient information. Please write to us instead.

**A file that will not open:** tell us the format and the scanner or microscope that wrote it.
Never send patient-identifiable images or data.
